Data Gathering

Demographics

We get the total students from BSGeology from Partido State University which has a 101 and get the sample size from it with the margin of error of 10%. But due to heat index while the study is taken, the course instructor or facilitator suggested to make the population smaller.

User Roles

Only student from BSGeology of Partido State University is the respondents because it alligns in our project from their program.

Technical Skills

All participants have moderate to high proficiency with web-based applications.

Consent Form

Survey Instrument

Information for this study was gathered through face-to-face, structured interviews conducted. This method was selected to ensure a high response rate and to allow the research team to clarify complex questions for participants in real-time. To maintain consistency, interviewers utilized a standardized survey instrument and followed a uniform script. All responses were recorded manually by the interviewer to ensure data integrity and completeness.
