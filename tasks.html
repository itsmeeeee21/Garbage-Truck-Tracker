Tasks & Scenarios

The Scenario

"You want to see the scheduled pick up on your barangay or barrio."

Schedule for today

  • Tap the map icon at the bottom of the Home page.
  • Click on calendar icon at the bottom.
  • Now, you can see the scheduled pick up for the week and for the whole month.

The Scenario

"You moved to the other barangay or municipality. You want to change the address where you are currently are."

Changing the Address

  • Tap MORE at the bottom of the Home page.
  • Navigate to My Account.
  • Click on Account Information, then Address.
  • Now, you can freely change the address if you moved to other place.
